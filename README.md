# How Do I Calculate How Many Plates to Put on the Bar

HDICHMPPB: A century-old problem. If I only have multiples of five kg up to 20, 1.5, and 2.5, there is no way to get 97 kg on the bar, right? Wrong! You put [20, 10, 2.5, 1.5, 1.5, 1.5, 1.5] kg on each side. It's gonna look ridiculous, but who cares. 

I made this because I was in the gym with a homie today and noticed that he thought that the greedy solution was the only way to go. Guess who is taking Algorithms and Data Structures 2 and just sat through a lecture on dynamic programming? This guy (me, not the other guy). Also, I just really wanted an excuse to try using the sentinel value from PEP 661. CC and I added a questionable amount of validation logic and tests, but I want to take credit for the idea to do so (that guy only writes tests that pass). I am adding a license for the love of the game.
